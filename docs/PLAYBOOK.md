# The Freedom Funnel Playbook

An affiliate marketing system inspired by the public teaching of **John Artzberger**
([@JohnArtzberger](https://www.youtube.com/@JohnArtzberger), *From Failure To Freedom*).
This is our own summary of the ideas he talks about publicly, not his official material.
Watch his channel for the source.

---

## 1. The philosophy in five lines

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **From failure to freedom** | Setbacks are training. John went from oilfield work and a marketing agency lost to COVID to building an online business. Your past does not decide your results. |
| **Free traffic first** | Use free short-form video (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reels) before you pay for ads. |
| **Bridge, don't direct-link** | Never send cold viewers straight to a sales page. Warm them up on your own page first. |
| **Own the list** | Social platforms can ban or bury you. An email list is an asset you own. The follow-up is where most sales happen. |
| **Consistency beats talent** | One simple system, done daily for 30+ days, beats five systems tried for five days. |

## 2. The Free Social Funnel

```
 Short video (free)  ->  Bio link  ->  Bridge page  ->  Email captured  ->  Affiliate offer
   TikTok / Shorts         tracked      your voice,      5-email            ClickBank,
   / Reels                 per source   video, bullets   follow-up          Digistore24, etc.
```

1. **Top of funnel - attention.** Post a 15-30 second video daily. Give real value or a real result.
2. **Middle of funnel - trust.** The bridge page explains, in your words, why the product helps. It offers a free bonus (guide, checklist) in exchange for an email.
3. **Bottom of funnel - sales.** The email sequence tells your story, handles objections and links to the offer.

The app automates steps 2 and 3, and tracks every step.

## 3. Picking offers

Good beginner offers are **digital products** on networks like **ClickBank** and **Digistore24**:

- 50-75% commission, no shipping, no inventory.
- Evergreen niches: health/weight loss, wealth/make money, relationships, hobbies (pets, gardening, fishing).
- Check: a decent gravity/sales rank, a solid sales page, refund rate not crazy, affiliate tools available.
- Pick **one niche** and **one offer** to start. Add more only when the first one gets sales.

## 4. The content engine (30-day sprint)

Make one video per day. The planner in the app rotates five hook styles:

| Hook type | Template example |
|---|---|
| Curiosity | "Nobody talks about this {niche} trick..." |
| Pain | "If {niche} keeps beating you, watch this" |
| Story | "A year ago I failed at {niche}. Here is what changed." |
| Listicle | "3 {niche} tips I wish I knew sooner" |
| "I tried it" | "I tried {offer} for 7 days. Honest results." |

**Script structure (30 seconds):**

1. **Hook (0-3s):** stop the scroll. Big text on screen.
2. **Value (3-20s):** one specific tip or result. Show it.
3. **Bridge (20-30s):** "I put the full breakdown on a free page."
4. **CTA:** "Link in bio" or "Comment INFO."

**Rules that help:**

- Faceless works: screen recordings, b-roll, text on screen and a voiceover.
- Same video can go to all three platforms; track each with its own source link.
- Look at the dashboard weekly. Make more of the hooks that get opt-ins, not just views.

## 5. Bridge page checklist

- Headline that matches the video's promise.
- Short video (optional) of you or your screen.
- 3-5 bullets of benefits.
- One field for email (name optional), one button.
- Clear affiliate disclosure.
- After opt-in the visitor goes straight to the offer and gets the welcome email.

## 6. Email follow-up (default sequence)

| Day | Email | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Here is what you asked for | Deliver the promise + link |
| 1 | How I went from failure to freedom | Story, build trust |
| 3 | The one thing most people get wrong | Teach, reframe |
| 5 | "Is it worth it?" | Handle objections |
| 7 | Last note on this | Final call to action |

After the sequence, keep emailing value weekly and promote a new offer once in a while.

## 7. Weekly rhythm

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Mon | Batch-film 3-4 videos from the content plan |
| Tue-Sun | Post 1 per day per platform, mark as **Posted** in the app |
| Wed | Reply to every comment; use comments for new hook ideas |
| Sun | Check the dashboard: views -> opt-ins -> clicks by source. Double down on what works. |

**Benchmarks to aim for:** bridge opt-in rate 20-40%; email click rate 3-10%.

## 8. Stay compliant

- **FTC:** disclose affiliate links on the bridge page, in emails and in video captions ("#ad" / "affiliate link in bio").
- **CAN-SPAM / GDPR:** real mailing address in every email, working unsubscribe (built in), only email people who opted in.
- **Platform rules:** some platforms limit links in bios; use the bridge page link only.
- **No fake claims:** do not promise income or health results you cannot back up.
- **Network rules:** read ClickBank/Digistore24 terms (some ban certain traffic types or email swipes).

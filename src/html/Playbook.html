The Free Social Funnel

Short video (free) → bio link → bridge page → email captured → affiliate offer. Inspired by John Artzberger's public teaching (From Failure To Freedom). Full version in docs/PLAYBOOK.md.

Five principles

  1. From failure to freedom. Setbacks are training. Your past does not decide your results.
  2. Free traffic first. TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Reels before paid ads.
  3. Bridge, don't direct-link. Warm viewers up on your own page before the sales page.
  4. Own the list. Platforms can bury you; your email list is yours. Follow-up makes the sales.
  5. Consistency beats talent. One system, every day, for 30+ days.

Your 30-day sprint

  1. Offers tab: add one digital offer (ClickBank / Digistore24) in one niche.
  2. Links tab: put the TikTok, Shorts and Reels bridge links in each bio.
  3. Content Plan tab: generate 30 days of hooks + scripts.
  4. Every day: film and post one video, mark it Posted.
  5. Emails tab: personalise the 5-email sequence with your own story.
  6. Every Sunday: check the Dashboard. Make more of what gets opt-ins.

30-second script

  • Hook (0-3s): stop the scroll, big text on screen.
  • Value (3-20s): one specific tip or result. Show it.
  • Bridge (20-30s): "I put the full breakdown on a free page."
  • CTA: "Link in bio" or "Comment INFO."

Benchmarks

  • Bridge opt-in rate: 20-40%
  • Email click rate: 3-10%

Stay compliant

  • Disclose affiliate links (bridge page, emails, captions).
  • Real mailing address + unsubscribe in every email (set in Settings; unsubscribe is automatic).
  • No income or health claims you cannot back up. Follow each network's traffic rules.
